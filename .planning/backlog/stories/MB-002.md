# MB-002: Employee identity and access

## Story

Employee identity and access enables the agreed meeting room workflow. Business rules: [specification](../../../docs/specs/booking-system.md).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Employees can sign in, rotate refresh tokens, sign out and change passwords.
2. Administrators manage accounts and cannot remove the last active administrator.
3. Disabled accounts lose access and credentials do not appear in responses or logs.
